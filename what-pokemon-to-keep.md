# What Pokémon to keep (and what to transfer)

**Snapshot date:** 1 September 2026 · **accuracy check:** 10 September 2026  
**One rule:** keep by **job**, not by star rating. A 15/15/15 is not automatically a keeper. A 0-attack 3-star of a Great League species often is.

Transfers cannot be undone. Tag or Favorite first, transfer last.

---

## 1. Decide the job before you look at IVs

Every Pokémon you keep should have at least one of these jobs:

| Job | What “good” means |
| --- | --- |
| **Raid / gym attacker** | High Attack IV, correct moves, Shadow if the species is a top DPS |
| **GO Battle League** | Right species + right IV spread **for that league** + two charged moves eventually |
| **Max Battles** | Dynamax / Gigantamax investment (particles spent live on **that** Pokémon). You must keep **≥1** Max Pokémon on the account |
| **Mega / Primal** | Energy already invested, or the species is your coverage Mega |
| **Showcase** | XXL (height is most of the score). XXS is medal/future-proof only right now |
| **Collection** | Shiny, costume, hundo, nundo, regional, Unown letter, Spinda pattern, living dex |
| **Trade / lucky bait** | Extra legendaries, 2016–2020 catches, 3-star trade rerolls |
| **Candy factory** | Only while you still need candy; then transfer |
| **HOME / GO Park** | One-way. Do not send your only copy of anything you still play in GO |

If it has no job, it is candy.

## 2. How IVs actually work

IVs are Attack / Defense / Stamina, each 0–15.

**Star rating** is a lumped IV percent. It is useful as a filter, not as a verdict.

### 2.1 IV floors (you cannot roll below these)

| Source | Floor |
| --- | --- |
| Wild | 0/0/0 (hundo 1 in 4,096) |
| Weather-boosted wild | 4/4/4 (hundo 1 in 1,728 — not 1,331) |
| Raids, eggs, most research | 10/10/10 (hundo 1 in 216) |
| **Shadow raids** | **6/6/6** |
| Friendship trades | Good 1 · Great 2 · Ultra 3 · Best 5, then reroll |
| Lucky trade | 12/12/12 (hundo 1 in 64) |
| GBL Battle Day / some championship weekends | Can drop toward 0/0/0 (this is how you farm 0-Attack PvP IVs) |
| Purify | +2 each IV, Pokémon set to level 25 |

Raid/egg/research Pokémon will never be Great League rank-1 on species that want 0 Attack. That is why wild catches, trades, Rocket shadows, and **GBL 0-floor events** matter for PvP.

**Hyper Training (Bottle Caps, mid-2025):** raise chosen IVs via Buddy tasks (Good Buddy+, ~365-day window). Gold = multiple stats, Silver = one. **Shadows and existing 4★s cannot be Hyper Trained.** Hyper-trained Pokémon **cannot go to HOME**. Raising Attack can push a GL/UL Pokémon over the CP cap. Use Bottle Caps on irreplaceable mythicals / one-copy legendaries, not farmable species. [GO Hub IVs](https://pokemongohub.net/post/guide/pokemon-go-iv-guide-explained/) · [Serebii Hyper Training](https://serebii.net/pokemongo/hypertraining.shtml).

### 2.2 Raids and Master League: high IVs win

No CP cap (Master League) or you want max DPS (raids): **15/15/15 (“hundo”) is best**. Attack IV matters most for raid DPS. A 15/14/14 raid attacker is fine. Do not sit on a 10/10/10 legendary forever if you will actually use it this season — but do not dump Stardust into a 10/10/10 if a better one is in raids next week.

### 2.3 Great League and Ultra League: low Attack often wins

Great League = 1,500 CP. Ultra League = 2,500 CP.

Attack inflates CP faster than Defense or Stamina. A high-Attack IV hits the cap at a **lower Pokémon level**, so it has less bulk. A low-Attack / high-Def / high-HP spread hits the same cap at a **higher level** and usually has a higher **stat product** (Attack × Defense × HP at the cap).

That is why **0/15/15** (or 0/15/14, 1/15/15, etc.) is the classic PvP hunt, **not** 15/15/15.

Exceptions:

- Species that **cannot reach the CP cap** even at level 50 (example often cited: Chansey in Great League) — then a hundo can be rank 1.
- You may want a slightly higher Attack to **win CMP** (charged-move ties go to higher Attack). That is an advanced tweak, not the default keep rule.
- **Lucky** Pokémon have a 12 Attack floor, so they are usually mediocre in GL/UL and excellent in Master League / raids.

Check the actual rank on [PvPoke](https://pvpoke.com/) or PokéGenie. “Rank 1” vs “Rank 80” is a small stat-product gap. **Species choice beats IV rank.** A rank-200 Azumarill still beats a rank-1 Rattata.

Community shorthand: ranks 1–50 of a meta species are investment-grade; “within ~1% of rank 1” is good enough below high Elo.

Sources: [Pokémon GO Hub PvP IVs](https://pokemongohub.net/post/pvp/ivs-understanding-and-ranking-them-in-pvp/), [POGO Cleanup — what to keep](https://pogocleanup.com/guides/what-to-keep-pokemon-go.html), [PvPoke rankings](https://pvpoke.com/rankings/all/1500/overall/).

## 3. Keep lists by category

### Always keep (until you are sure)

- **Shinies** (game also blocks bulk transfer; still Favorite them)
- **Mythicals** (often one per account)
- **Legendaries / Ultra Beasts** until you have decided raid vs Master vs trade vs candy
- **Hundos (4\*)**
- **Nundos (0/0/0)** if you collect them — they are rarer than hundos in the wild
- **Lucky** Pokémon (half Stardust; 12+ floor)
- **Costumes / event backgrounds / hats** — keep **one of each variant you care about**
- **Unown letters** (A–Z, `!`, `?`) and **Spinda patterns** (nine; Form 9 often Valentine-locked). Keep **one of each form**, plus shinies. Spinda is frequently blocked from HOME.
- Anything with a **legacy / Community Day / Elite TM** move (`@special` search)
- Pokémon you have spent **Mega Energy, Max Particles, or fusion materials** on. **Shadows and purified cannot fuse** (Necrozma / Kyurem).
- **Best Buddy** and active buddy candidates
- **Dynamax / Gigantamax** copies you will actually use. You **cannot transfer/trade your last Max Pokémon**. Gigantamax trades are always Special Trades. G-Max cannot go to HOME. Max Move levels reset on trade.

### Raid attackers — keep the tools, not every 3-star

Build **type coverage**, not a living museum of 3-star Rhydons.

Priority stack per type (philosophy, not a forever tier list):

1. **Mega / Primal** of that type (lobby boost)
2. **Shadow** of a top species (**+20% attack**; cannot be traded without purifying)
3. Non-shadow legendary / origin / fusion formes
4. Cheap community-day attackers (Mamoswine, Metagross, Swampert, etc.)

Keep **3–6** attackers per type you raid, plus 1 Mega. Transfer the 2-star leftovers.

Shadow rule: a mediocre-IV Shadow of a meta attacker often out-DPS a non-shadow hundo. **Do not purify** Shadow Mewtwo, Rayquaza, Groudon, Machamp, Metagross, Mamoswine, Chandelure, etc. unless you have a specific PvP purify reason.

Frustration: only removable during Rocket TM windows. Keep the Shadow; wait for the window.

### PvP — keep by league

Search candidates with something like:

```
0-1attack&3-4defense&3-4hp
```

Then check rank for **that species in that league**. Tag `GL`, `UL`, `ML`.

How many copies:

| League | Typical keep |
| --- | --- |
| Great League | 1–2 of each species you play (sometimes a CMP variant) |
| Ultra League | 1 of each you will fund; UL is expensive |
| Master League | Best IVs / lucky / shadow of legendaries you will actually max |
| Little Cup | 1 unevolved with good PvP IVs **if you play the cup** |

Do **not** power up a GL Pokémon until you have checked it will stay under 1,500 at the target level.

**Second charged move** is part of “keeping for PvP.” A PvP Pokémon with one move is unfinished. Unlock the second move **before** dumping XL candy into it if you are still unsure.

### Gym defenders

Keep 1–2 high-HP walls (Blissey, Snorlax, Chansey, Giratina, Togekiss) if you farm the 50 daily coins. IVs barely matter compared to “is it still in the gym.”

### Collection / dex

- Living dex: one of each species/form you care about
- Gender if you are a completionist
- **Meltan / Melmetal** via Mystery Box (HOME / Let’s GO) — keep a hundo and a PvP Melmetal if you play Master Premier / UL
- **Galarian birds** from Daily Incense — keep all until you have the shiny/hundo you want; they are rare
- **Trade-evolve** leftovers (`tradeevolve`) — keep until traded

### Dynamax / Gigantamax

Investment is on the individual. Keep:

- Your leveled Max Move attackers
- At least one healer / tank if the current Max meta needs it
- Transfer extra unlevelled Dynamax commons after you have a usable copy

Search: `dynamax`, `gigantamax`

## 4. How many of a legendary is “enough”?

For a species like Mewtwo:

1. Best IV **raid** copy (high Attack)
2. **Shadow** if you have one
3. **Shiny**
4. **Lucky** if you will power it up
5. Maybe one **Master League** hundo (often the same as #1)
6. Maybe one **GL/UL** IV spread if the species is actually used there

Trade a couple of extras with Best Friends (IV reroll + lucky chance). Transfer the rest for candy / XL. There is no medal for 12 bad Mewtwos.

## 5. Special cases people mess up

| Case | Keep? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shadow | Keep meta attackers; transfer junk | Cannot trade without purify (loses +20% Atk) |
| Purified | Usually worse for raids | +2 IVs, cheaper power-up, Return; useful for gems / some PvP |
| 2016–2020 catches | Trade bait before transfer | Higher lucky chance |
| Costume Pikachu | One of each you like | Often cannot evolve |
| XXL / XXS | Keep XXL for Showcases (height ~80% of score) | XXS: Tiny Collector medal / future-proof; no current smallest-showcase |
| Fusion (Necrozma, Kyurem) | Keep fused + the unfused bases | **Shadow and purified cannot fuse** |
| Hyper-trained | Keep | Cannot send to HOME |
| Adventure Effect legendaries | Keep the move | Walking-effect moves are limited |
| Traded Pokémon | Check new IVs | Trades reroll; a Pokémon can be traded **only once** |
| HOME transfer | One-way | Don’t send your only hundo. Shadows, G-Max, Hyper-trained, many costumes cannot go |
| Galarian Articuno / Zapdos / Moltres | **Keep every catch** | Daily Adventure Incense only; shiny generally does not flee but can still fail the catch |
| 2016–2020 of useful species | Lucky-trade bait | Official NY 2026: in storage since 2020 = guaranteed Lucky until **45** such trades |

**Old “transfer everything under 3 stars” advice is harmful.** It deletes Great League rank-1s.

## 6. Practical inventory workflow

Goal: never transfer a keeper by accident.

1. Favorite or tag irreplaceables:

   ```
   4*,shiny,lucky,legendary,mythical,ultrabeast
   ```

2. Tag mechanics:

   ```
   dynamax,gigantamax,mega1,mega2,mega3,fusion
   ```

3. Tag PvP candidates, then check ranks:

   ```
   0-1attack&3-4defense&3-4hp
   ```

4. Tag raid families you care about with high Attack.

5. Review **untagged only**:

   ```
   !#
   ```

6. Safe-ish mass-transfer view (still eyeball it):

   ```
   !4*&!shiny&!shadow&!legendary&!mythical&!lucky&!costume&!background&!dynamax&!gigantamax&!favorite&!#
   ```

After Community Day, restrict to recent catches: `age0-7`.

Pin searches in the search bar. Use tags (`PVP`, `RAID`, `MAX`, `MEGA`, `DEX`, `TRADE`) up to the game’s tag cap. Full paste-ready strings: [in-game-filters.md](in-game-filters.md).

## 7. What is safe to transfer

- Duplicate wild commons with no PvP spread and no 3-star raid stats
- Extra evolutions after the dex entry
- Event chaff you caught for a quest
- Shadows of non-meta species (purify cheap ones only if you need the purify medal / gems)
- Extra unevolved after you have the evolved attacker and the PvP pre-evo if needed

When in doubt, Favorite and leave it until next cleanup.

## 9. Buddy and XL candy

- Walk the species you will power past 40. Legendaries are 20 km/candy unless reduced by events / best buddy / Routes.
- Rare Candy XL is for legendaries and mythicals, not for Pidgey.
- Community Day and Spotlight Hour (catch or transfer bonus) are when you generate XL for a line.
- Do not Best Buddy a Pokémon you will transfer. Hearts are time.

## 10. Quick decision tree

```
Is it shiny, mythical, costume-you-like, hundo, lucky, or fused/mega/max-invested?
  YES → keep (at least one)
Is it a Shadow of a raid/PvP species?
  YES → keep until you have a better Shadow; do not purify by default
Is the species in this season’s GL/UL meta?
  YES → check PvP rank; keep top ~100 IV spreads you might play
Is it a top raid type attacker with 13+ Attack (or any usable Shadow)?
  YES → keep until you have 3–6 better
Do you still need the dex / XL / trade?
  YES → keep temporarily, tag it
Else → transfer for candy
```
