Box Coach

Companion notes · not a game client

Stays on the allowed side of the fairness policy. This app never logs into Pokémon GO, never reads your location, and never taps the game for you. Official auto-catch/spin is Explorer Gadget or GO Plus+ inside the real app. Live events: Leek Duck · PvP ranks: PvPoke.

Sort on your phone

Copy → paste in GO → tag or transfer. Setup is on Tags. Open the DIY the first time; use Steps when you already know the loop.

What this system is

You are not cleaning “bad IVs.” You are giving every keeper a job, then transferring anything with no job. The star (Favorite) is the only thing that blocks a mass-transfer. Tags do not. Star irreplaceables and tag the ones you will use.

Work in two apps: Box Coach (this page) and Pokémon GO storage. Copy here, switch to GO, paste in the search bar, act, switch back. On iPhone: swipe up and hold, tap GO, paste. After the first pin, you can stay in GO and tap the named favorite.

Do this once

1. Create 8 tags in GO

Storage → Tags → + . Type the name exactly. Never name a tag shiny, lucky, or shadow — those are GO keywords. RAID and MEGA are fine; always search them as #RAID and #MEGA.

  • PVP — 1–2 Great/Ultra League copies per species you play. Low Attack, high bulk. Not RAID on this same copy.
  • RAID — highest Attack / best Shadow. One winner per species. Do not purify meta Shadows.
  • MAX — Dynamax / G-Max you will actually level.
  • MEGA — one coverage Mega or Primal you will energy-dump into.
  • DEX — one living-dex / costume. This is the only tag that may stack on the PVP or RAID winner.
  • TRADE — extras for friends. Never also PVP or RAID.
  • WALL — 1–2 gym defenders (Chansey, Blissey, Snorlax…).
  • TEMP — dump later (event, showcase). Remove the tag when you transfer.

Default: one job tag per Pokémon. Two copies when RAID IVs and PVP IVs disagree (a 15-Atk raid Machamp and a bulk PVP Machamp). Two tags on one copy only for DEX + combat.

2. Pin 12 searches

In GO storage, paste a string, search, then long-press that recent search → Favorite. Rename it to the Box Coach name (WEEK, INBOX, PVPIV…). You get 12 favorites. Unpin anything leftover from older versions (DUPERAID, UL2500 without !#).

On the Tags tab: Name copies the label, Copy copies the string. Do this for all 12, in order 1–12.

3. How to act on a list in GO

  1. Paste (or tap the pin) in the search bar. The list filters live.
  2. Long-press one Pokémon until the checkboxes appear.
  3. Tap more copies, or Select all only if you have scrolled the whole list.
  4. Bottom menu: Tag (job), Favorite (star), Transfer, or Evolve.
  5. Clear the search (X) before the next pin so you do not stack filters by accident.

Rules that save you from mistakes

  • !# = no custom tag. Hunts hide tagged copies so the same Chikorita does not come back.
  • evolve = enough candy (and items) to evolve right now, not “fully evolved.” A Chikorita with <25 candy matches !evolve.
  • The + family prefix only works at the start of a search or after a comma. That is why GL1500 and EVOCD put family names first.
  • GO truncates at ~200 characters. Do not add extra families onto GL1500.
  • Low Attack + high Defense/HP is a screen, not PvPoke rank-1. Skip species you will not play. Confirm on PvPoke before XL.
  • Never name a search pin the same as a job tag in a confusing way: the gold chip PVPIV is a search name; the tag on the Pokémon is PVP.

Step 1 — This week’s pile

Pin WEEK. After a walk or Spotlight, this is the only list you need. INBOX is the same idea for the whole box (first cleanup, or when WEEK looks empty but storage is still full).

  1. Copy WEEK → paste in GO. You should see only Pokémon caught in the last 7 days that have no job tag.
  2. Scroll once. Star every shiny, 4★, lucky, legendary, mythical, costume, and anything you have already powered or Maxed. Star first. Tagging without a star can still be mass-transferred later if you untag.
  3. Still in this list, tag obvious jobs as you go:
    • Dynamax / G-Max you will use → MAX
    • A Mega you will actually energy → MEGA
    • Costume / living-dex copy → DEX (can also sit on a PVP or RAID winner)
    • Spare legendary for a friend → TRADE
    • Gym wall → WALL
    • Not sure, but not candy today → TEMP
  4. Leave junk untagged. Do not tag “maybe PVP” yet — that is Step 2.
  5. When the list is only stuff you are willing to turn into candy (or empty of keepers), tap Next.

If this is your first time ever, run INBOX instead of WEEK so old untagged Pokémon get the same pass.

Step 2 — Tag PVP

Two pins, both untagged-only. After you tag a copy PVP, it disappears from both lists. That is the point.

PVPIV first

  1. Copy PVPIV. Paste. This is every untagged Pokémon with Attack IV 0–5 and Defense/HP 11–15.
  2. This list is noisy. Skip anything you will not play in Great or Ultra League (Rattata, random first-stages, stuff you hate using).
  3. Skip Chikorita / Bayleef unless you actually want a Meganium. Same idea for every baby/first stage.
  4. Per species you will play: pick the best 1–2 bulk spreads. Long-press → Tag → PVP. Leave extras untagged.
  5. Do not also tag that copy RAID. If the same species has a 15-Attack raid winner, that is a second Pokémon in Step 3.
  6. Do not power up yet. Great League must stay ≤ 1,500 CP. Check the IVs tab or PvPoke before XL.

GL1500 second

  1. Copy GL1500. This is the same IV idea, but only common Great League families (Azumarill, Medicham, Swampert, and the rest on the pin), CP ≤ 1,500, still untagged.
  2. Tag PVP on the winner if you did not already. If the list is empty, you already tagged them in PVPIV — good.
  3. Ultra League: do not pin UL2500 unless you play UL every week. Type it from the Search tab when you need it: untagged, CP 1,501–2,500, same bulk IVs.

Step 3 — Tag RAID

Raids want 15 Attack, not PvP bulk. Shadow of a meta attacker beats a non-shadow hundo. Do not purify by default.

RAID15

  1. Copy RAID15. Untagged 15-Attack 3★ and 4★.
  2. One winner per species — not six 15-Atk Rhydon. Tag RAID on the best (highest CP / already evolved / Shadow if you have one).
  3. If a worse copy is already tagged RAID from last week, open #RAID later and untag the loser.

SHADATK

  1. Copy SHADATK. Every untagged Shadow.
  2. Meta (Mewtwo, Machamp, Metagross, Mamoswine, Rayquaza, Garchomp, Dragonite…) → Tag RAID. Keep Frustration until a Rocket TM window.
  3. Junk Shadows (Pidgey, Whismur, random 1★) → leave untagged. They become candy in Step 5. Do not purify them “to save.”

FRUST

  1. Copy FRUST when a Rocket event is on, or if SHADATK was huge.
  2. This is untagged Shadows that still know Frustration. Tag RAID on the meta ones you missed. Skip the rest.

Step 4 — Evolve tagged only

Candy is the scarce resource. You evolve the tagged winner, never the untagged extra sitting next to it with 124 candy.

  1. Copy EVOTAG. This is RAID / PVP / DEX / MEGA / MAX that can evolve right now (enough candy).
  2. Skip Little Cup PVP copies (you wanted them unevolved). If you tagged one by mistake, untag or skip it in this list.
  3. Evolve from this list only. Queue a few, don’t select-all if the list is mixed.
  4. Community Day: Copy EVOCD instead (or after). That is those CD families and already tagged RAID or PVP. Untagged extras in the same family will not appear — that is how you avoid evolving the junk Marshtomp.
  5. If EVOTAG is empty, you either have no candy or everything is already final form. That is fine. Power-up and second moves are not this step — do those on #PVP / #RAID later from the Search tab.

Step 5 — Transfer leftovers

If it still has no star and no job tag, it is candy. Scroll every list. There is no undo.

  1. EXTRAS — untagged extras of a species you already own two of, and not Favorited. These are the duplicates of a winner you already tagged. Transfer.
  2. AGE07 — this week’s leftovers that are not 3★/4★, not shiny/shadow/lucky/legendary, not starred, not tagged. Spotlight / CD chaff. Still eyeball costumes and anything you like.
  3. DUMP — only if storage is still tight. Hides 4★, shiny, shadow, legendary, mythical, lucky, costume, background, Dynamax, G-Max, favorites, and tagged. Whatever remains is the aggressive pile. Scroll it. Then transfer.
  4. Never use DUMP3 until PVP tags exist. It hides 3★ and will delete Great League rank-1s.
  5. Never Select all on a transfer list you have not scrolled. One starred legendary with a failed tag is enough to ruin the night.

When you are done, INBOX (!#) should be a short list of “I have not decided.” Tag TEMP or star those. A clean box is: starred, tagged, or gone.

After the sort (not every session)

  • Second charged move on PVP before you dump XL: Search tab → PVP2MV (#PVP&!@3move).
  • Power RAID that cannot evolve further: PWRRAID.
  • Confirm a PVP spend on PvPoke. Species choice beats IV rank.
  • Stuck on one Pokémon: use the wizard at the bottom of Steps.

If the list looks wrong

  • Same Pokémon as last search — the pin is missing !#. Re-Copy from Tags. After you tag, they should vanish.
  • Chikorita on a Great League search — PVPIV is every bulk IV. Skip it. GL1500 should not include Chikorita (not a core family). If it does, you pasted the old CP-only string.
  • GL1500 shows whole families with bad IVs — you put + after &. Re-Copy GL1500; families must come first.
  • EVOCD evolves extras — old string had no #RAID,#PVP. Re-Copy EVOCD.
  • Search named RAID shows raid-caught Pokémon — type #RAID for the tag. The word raid means caught in a raid.
  • Nothing in WEEK — everything from this week is already tagged, or you have not caught in 7 days. Use INBOX.

Official ways to play with the phone in your pocket

If you wanted automation, use what the publisher ships: Explorer Gadget (level 20+, Poké Balls only, daily caps, cannot run with Plus+) or Pokémon GO Plus+. Those stay inside the official app. Third-party clickers, GPS joysticks, and modified clients are how accounts get the three-strike ban.

Competitive habits

Play the event calendar. Finish one Great League team (two charged moves) before you dump Stardust into random 3-stars. Shadows: do not purify meta attackers. Species choice beats IV rank.
