# Pokémon GO overview

**Snapshot date:** 1 September 2026 · **accuracy check:** 10 September 2026  
**Scope:** How the game works, what systems matter, and how a typical trainer spends a week.  
**Not this doc:** Keep-vs-transfer rules ([what-pokemon-to-keep.md](what-pokemon-to-keep.md)) or a competitive playbook ([how-to-win-competitive.md](how-to-win-competitive.md)).

**Live now (10 Sep 2026):** **Twilight Trails** (8 Sep–1 Dec 2026). GBL **8–15 Sep**: Great / Ultra / Master Mega Edition + 4× win Stardust. Community Day Classic **12 Sep**. Daily Discoveries are back on (Max Monday, Showcase Tuesday, Raid Hour Wed, GBL Thursday, etc.).

Pokémon GO is a live location-based game. You walk the real world, catch Pokémon, spin PokéStops and Gyms, and team up for raids, Max Battles, and GO Battle League. Ten years in, it is less a simple catch-em-all app and more a stack of overlapping systems: catching, combat, social, events, and a real-money economy.

---

## 1. What it is, and who runs it

- **Launch:** July 2016. Built by Niantic with The Pokémon Company.
- **Publisher today:** Scopely acquired Niantic’s games business (Pokémon GO, Pikmin Bloom, Monster Hunter Now, Campfire, Wayfarer) in **May 2025**. The geospatial/tech side spun out as **Niantic Spatial**. In **July 2026** the games team rebranded as **Scopely Explore** (a team inside Scopely, not a separate company). The Pokémon Company partnership continues.
- **Core loop:** Walk → encounter Pokémon and stops → catch / spin / battle → spend Stardust, Candy, and items to grow a box → repeat around a seasonal event calendar.
- **AR is optional.** Most competitive and efficient play uses the non-AR catch and battle screens. AR is flavor, not the main game.

Sources: [Official Scopely move post](https://pokemongo.com/post/moving-to-a-new-home-2025), [Scopely Explore announcement](https://www.scopely.com/en/news/introducing-scopely-explore), [Wikipedia: Niantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niantic,_Inc.).

## 2. The map and the daily world

| Map object | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Wild Pokémon** | Catch. Weather, events, incense, lures, and Routes change what appears. |
| **PokéStops** | Spin for items, Field Research, gifts. Some host Team GO Rocket, Showcases, or lures. |
| **Gyms** | Defend for PokéCoins, battle, and host raids. |
| **Power Spots** | Max Battles (Dynamax / Gigantamax). Purple X on the map. |
| **Routes** | Follow a path for bonus spawns, Mateo gift exchanges, and buddy candy distance cuts. Unlocked at trainer level 7; creating Routes at 30. |
| **Campfire overlay** | Flares and Team Up for finding nearby raid groups. |
| **Community Campsites** | Ambassador-community hubs with dedicated stops/gyms (rolling out from 2025). Not a camping minigame. |

**Explorer Gadget** (phased rollout, August 2026): first-party auto-catch / auto-spin with the app closed. Level 20+, Poké Balls only, daily caps by trainer level. **Cannot run at the same time as a paired GO Plus +.** It fills storage while you walk — tag keepers or you will transfer later in a panic. Help: [What is the Explorer Gadget?](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/5493-what-is-the-explorer-gadget/) · [Leek Duck](https://leekduck.com/posts/explorer-gadget/).

**Weather** is tied to real local weather. Matching types spawn more, give extra Stardust (community: 100 → 125), have a **4/4/4 IV floor**, and deal extra damage in PvE (community: ~1.2–1.25×). Weather-boosted raid bosses are higher CP. Type chart:

| Weather | Boosted types |
| --- | --- |
| Sunny / Clear | Grass, Fire, Ground |
| Rainy | Water, Electric, Bug |
| Partly cloudy | Normal, Rock |
| Cloudy | Fairy, Fighting, Poison |
| Windy | Flying, Dragon, Psychic |
| Snow | Ice, Steel |
| Fog | Dark, Ghost |

Official: [Weather boosts](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/53-weather-boosts/).

**Incense** attracts Pokémon to you. **Daily Adventure Incense** (from level 4) is the free daily version and is the main way many players hunt Galarian legendary birds — use it **while moving**. **Lure Modules** attract Pokémon to a PokéStop for everyone nearby; special lures (Mossy, Glacial, Magnetic, Rainy) also unlock some evolutions.

## 3. Catching

Catch chance is multiplicative. Stacking Ultra Ball + curveball + Golden Razz + Excellent throw is how legendaries get caught. Type medals also multiply catch rate (Platinum is **1.4×** for that type). Research encounters are easier than wild encounters of the same species.

Practical habits:

- Always curveball.
- Use Pinap / Silver Pinap on candy you need; Golden Razz on legendaries and anything that will run.
- Excellent throws matter more than people think, both for catch rate and XP.

Catch-rate reference: [Bulbapedia — Catch rate (GO)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Catch_rate_(GO)).

## 4. Combat Power, IVs, and levels (the numbers on a Pokémon)

Three things make a Pokémon strong:

1. **Species base stats** (fixed).
2. **IVs** — Attack, Defense, Stamina, each 0–15.
3. **Pokémon level** — raised with Stardust + Candy. Max is **level 50** (trainer must be 40+). Best Buddy adds one extra level while that buddy is active (**level 51**).

**Trainer level** (you) and **Pokémon level** (the creature) are different. In October 2025 the **trainer** cap rose from 50 to **80**. Pokémon combat math did not change.

**IV floors** (minimum IVs from a source):

| Source | Floor |
| --- | --- |
| Wild | 0/0/0 |
| Weather-boosted wild | 4/4/4 |
| Raids, eggs, most research | 10/10/10 |
| **Shadow raids** | **6/6/6** (not the 10 floor) |
| Friendship trades | Good 1s · Great 2s · Ultra 3s · Best 5s (then full reroll above the floor) |
| Lucky trade | 12/12/12 |
| GBL Battle Day / some championship bonuses | Can drop toward 0/0/0 so low-Attack PvP spreads exist |
| Purify | +2 each IV, Pokémon set to level 25 |

**Hyper Training** (Bottle Caps, mid-2025): raise chosen IVs via Buddy tasks. Shadows and existing hundos are ineligible. Hyper-trained Pokémon **cannot go to HOME**. Do not Hyper Train a Great League Pokémon over the CP cap. Guide: [GO Hub IVs](https://pokemongohub.net/post/guide/pokemon-go-iv-guide-explained/).

Appraisal stars in-game are a rough IV summary, not a keep/transfer verdict. A 3-star with a Great League spread can beat a 4-star of the same species in PvP. Details: [what-pokemon-to-keep.md](what-pokemon-to-keep.md).

## 5. Trainer progression (level 1–80)

- **Levels 1–70:** XP only.
- **Levels 71–80:** XP **plus** Level-Up Research tasks (platinum medals, km walked, raids, GBL, etc.).
- **Level 40** is still the gate to power Pokémon to level 50.
- **Candy XL** unlocks at trainer level 31 (needed for Pokémon levels 41–50).
- High-level unlocks include Routes (7), Team GO Rocket (8), GBL and trading (10), Mega Evolution (18), Max Battles (20), Party Play (21), create Routes (30), nominate PokéStops (35), increased Lucky Friend chance (70).

XP shortcuts: Lucky Egg + mass evolutions; Lucky Egg + Best Friend unlocks (100k XP each, 200k with egg); raid XP during events; Excellent throws.

Sources: [Leek Duck level-cap post](https://leekduck.com/posts/pokemon-go-level-cap-increase-80/), [Pokémon GO Hub level 1–80 guide](https://pokemongohub.net/post/guide/pokemon-go-level-1-to-80-guide-xp-level-up-tasks-and-rewards/) (updated 27 Aug 2026).

## 6. The resource economy

These are the currencies that actually gate progress:

| Resource | What it buys | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stardust** | Power-ups, second moves, trades | Catching, GBL, gifts, Adventure Sync, events, Star Pieces |
| **Candy / Candy XL** | Evolve and power up a species | Catch, transfer, walk buddy, raids, Rare Candy |
| **Rare Candy / Rare Candy XL** | Convert to any species’ candy | Raids, GBL, research, Adventure Sync |
| **PokéCoins** | Storage, raid passes, incubators, boxes | Gym defense (cap **50/day**) or real money |
| **Raid / Remote / Premium passes** | Raids | Free daily Gym spin; shop; events |
| **Max Particles** | Max Battles | Power Spots, walking; soft cap raised (community: ~1,000 → **1,500** in May 2025 — confirm in-game) |
| **Mega / Primal Energy** | Mega Evolve / Primal Reversion | Mega/Primal raids, walking a mega-capable buddy after first mega |
| **TMs / Elite TMs** | Change moves | Raids, GBL, events. Elite TMs teach exclusive/legacy moves |

**Bag and storage** fill constantly. Level-ups now grant extra Pokémon/item storage. Buying storage is one of the few shop purchases that stays useful forever.

**Gym coins:** 1 coin per 10 minutes a defender holds a gym, **50 coin daily cap** across all gyms. Spread defenders rather than stacking one gym. High-HP Pokémon (Blissey, Snorlax, Chansey, Togekiss) drain motivation slower.

## 7. Major gameplay systems

### 7.1 Gyms and raids

Raids are timed co-op boss fights at Gyms. Win, then catch the boss with Premier Balls (or Beast Balls for Ultra Beasts).

Common formats in 2026:

- **1-star / 3-star / 5-star (legendary)**
- **Mega / Mega Legendary / Super Mega**
- **Primal** (Kyogre / Groudon energy)
- **Shadow raids** (enrage + Purified Gems; 8 gems to fully subdue, max 5 per trainer)
- **Ultra Beast** (Beast Balls)
- **Elite raids** (hard, short window, typically in-person only)

**Pass rule of thumb:** the free daily pass is for a raid you care about (usually a 5-star or mega), not a random 1-star. Remote passes: inventory cap commonly **3**; daily use raised to **10** on 13 May 2025 (events can raise it further). Leave a bad lobby **before** the fight starts.

Premier Ball count goes up with damage dealt, gym control, and **friendship**. Official Premier Ball bonuses: Great +1, Ultra +2, Best **+4**. Raid with the same friends every week.

Shadow raids: when the boss enrages (~1/3 HP), it hits much harder and tanks more. Purified Gems (crafted from Shadow Shards from Rocket battles) subdue it. Two trainers with gems is the practical minimum for harder Shadow raids. Remote Shadow raids have been allowed since **13 May 2025**.

**Super Mega raids (2026):** harder Mega with shields that Mega Charged Attacks break. **Link Charges** (key item, Link Holder) are the in-person Super Mega currency alongside Premium Battle Passes. Remote Super Mega typically needs a Remote Pass **plus** Link Charges. Help: [Link Charges](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/5380-what-are-link-charges-and-how-do-i-collect-them/). Community-reported charge costs and holder caps **conflict** — check in-game.

### 7.2 Max Battles (Dynamax / Gigantamax)

Fought at **Power Spots**, not Gyms. Only Dynamax or Gigantamax Pokémon can enter. Up to **4** trainers per Dynamax battle; Gigantamax can scale much larger (groups of 4, up to ~40 in some reports).

- Spend **Max Particles** to start. Particles are typically only deducted on a **win**, so failed attempts can be retried.
- Fill a Max Meter, then Dynamax/Gigantamax for 3 turns.
- If you faint in 3-star+ Max Battles you **cannot rejoin with a new team**; you Cheer instead.
- After a win you can station a Pokémon at the Power Spot for candy and to boost later trainers.
- **Max Mushrooms** boost damage for 30 minutes.
- Roles: **Attacker** (Max Attack / G-Max), **Defender** (Max Guard), **Healer** (Max Spirit). Charged attacks in the non-Max phase are a common beginner mistake.
- You **must keep at least one** Max Pokémon on the account. Max Move progress **resets on trade**. Gigantamax cannot go to HOME. You cannot Mega and Dynamax the same Pokémon at once. Max Moves do **not** work in GBL.
- Remote Max Battles: Remote Pass + Particles since **19 May 2025**. Particle cap has been raised (community: 1,000 → 1,500). **Max Monday** is the weekly Power Spot cadence.

Official: [Max Battles help](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/4798-max-battles-and-catching-max-pokemon-1729887131/), [What are Max Pokémon?](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/4795-what-are-max-pokemon-1729886792/).

### 7.3 GO Battle League (PvP)

Remote 3v3 trainer battles. Leagues:

| League | CP cap |
| --- | --- |
| Little Cup (when in rotation) | 500, unevolved |
| Great League | 1,500 |
| Ultra League | 2,500 |
| Master League | none |

Weekly **themed cups** rotate beside the open leagues. **Mega Editions** (new in 2026) allow Megas; in GL/UL Mega CP is temporarily reduced for the battle.

**Season 28** (*Twilight Trails*) is live **8 September–1 December 2026** (GBL opened 8 Sep 1:00 p.m. PDT). Week of **8–15 Sep**: Great / Ultra / Master Mega Edition + 4× win Stardust. Rank structure is commonly Ranks 1–20 by battles/wins, then Ace 2000 / Veteran 2500 / Expert 2750 / Legend 3000. Wiki tables conflict — **trust the in-game Battle screen**.

Daily volume: **5 sets × 5 = 25**. **GO Battle Thursday:** **10 sets / 50 battles** and up to **4× Stardust on win rewards**. GBL Timed Research each season can award Elite TMs for battle/win milestones.

See [how-to-win-competitive.md](how-to-win-competitive.md).

### 7.4 Team GO Rocket

- **Grunts** at darkened PokéStops and balloons → Shadow Pokémon.
- **Leaders** Arlo, Cliff, Sierra via Rocket Radar (6 Mysterious Components).
- **Giovanni** via Super Rocket Radar. In 2026 “Taken Over” events this often comes from the **GO Pass** (Deluxe instant vs free-track rank), not the old Special Research loop. Official example: [Flying Taxi: Taken Over](https://pokemongolive.com/news/flying-taxi-taken-over-2026). Giovanni is typically **PokéStops only** (decoys exist); dual radars can conflict.

Shadows deal **+20% damage** and take more damage. They come with **Frustration**, which can only be TMed off during Rocket events. **Shadows cannot Mega, cannot Hyper Train, cannot fuse** (Necrozma/Kyurem). Do not purify a top raid attacker just to “make it nicer.”

### 7.5 Mega Evolution and Primals

Temporary form with higher stats. The raid-relevant effect is the **lobby boost**: same-type attacks ~**1.3×**, other ally attacks ~**1.1×**. Bring a Mega even if it is not your highest DPS.

**Super Max** Mega Level arrived with GO Tour: Kalos (Feb/Mar 2026). Official perks: much higher Mega CP, extra catch Candy/XP/XL for matching types, rest period before a free Mega drops to **~24 hours**. Super Max is a large Mega Energy sink. Energy-to-unlock numbers in blogs conflict — verify in-game. Official: [Mega 2026 update](https://pokemongolive.com/news/mega-evolution-2026-update).

You cannot Mega Evolve while Dynamax is active on that Pokémon.

### 7.6 Friends, gifts, trading, luckies

Official friendship points: Friend (accept) → Good (1) → Great (7) → Ultra (30) → Best (90) → **Forever (180)**. Forever Friends (global **11 Dec 2025**) unlock **one Remote Trade per day** with that friend. Extra remote trades cost another 90 friendship points each. Remote trades are **asynchronous** (tag, wish list, 48-hour steps) and official help says they **do not count as Special Trades**. Help: [Trading remotely](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/5312-trading-remotely/).

- Gifts: Stardust, items, 7 km eggs, postcards. Base open cap is commonly 30/day (GO Pass can raise it).
- **Special Trade** (shiny / legendary / costume / Gigantamax, etc.): **one per day** unless an event adds more.
- Trades **re-roll IVs**. Lucky trades guarantee 12/12/12 and **half Stardust** to power up.
- Official New Year 2026: Pokémon in storage **since 2020** can be **guaranteed Lucky**, cap **45** such guarantees (raised from 35). [NY 2026](https://pokemongolive.com/en/news/new-years-2026).
- Level 70+ trainers get an increased Lucky Friend chance.

### 7.7 Eggs, Adventure Sync, buddy

Egg distances: 2 / 5 / 7 (gifts) / 10 / 12 km (Strange Eggs from Rocket Leaders). Eggs have a 10/10/10 IV floor.

**Adventure Sync** tracks walking with the app closed (Apple Health / Google Fit). Weekly km thresholds (reset Monday local) pay balls, Stardust, and exclusive egg pools at 25 km and 50 km. 50 km/week is the realistic “I play this game” target.

**Buddy:** walk for candy, earn hearts, climb to Best Buddy for the +1 level combat boost. Excited mood doubles daily hearts.

**Daily Adventure Egg** (level 15): 1 km hatch in its own incubator, starter-line pool.

### 7.8 Research and events

- **Field Research** from stops → stamp → monthly Research Breakthrough.
- **Special Research** (story / Rocket / tickets).
- **Timed Research** (events).

**Daily Discoveries** (from Memories in Motion, refined in Forever Forward / Twilight Trails) replace “one weekly bonus” with themed days. They **turn off** during Global GO Fest / Wild Area / Tour / Mega Finale weeks.

| Day | Discovery (when active) | Play this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun | Scenic Sunday | Routes, Mateo, buddy candy |
| Mon | Max Monday | Power Spots / Dynamax |
| Tue | Showcase Tuesday | Up to 5 PokéStop Showcases (XXL) |
| Wed | Raid Hour 6–7 p.m. | In-person 5-star / Mega |
| Thu | GO Battle Thursday + Spotlight 6–7 p.m. | 10 GBL sets; mass spawn |
| Fri | Friendship Friday | Extra special trades, XL from trades (often **in-person**) |
| Sat | Often Community Day | Featured species + exclusive move |

Community Day exclusive moves: evolve **during** the window or the short post-event window, or spend an Elite Charged TM later.

**Near-term calendar:** Twilight Trails Community Days 12 Sep / 10 Oct / 21 Nov; City Safari 26–27 Sep (Boston, Brisbane, Lisbon, Marseille, Munich, Rio); Wild Area in-person Sendai + Mexico City 6–8 Nov; Wild Area Global 14–15 Nov (ticketed). GO Fest 2026 Global (11–12 Jul) was **free** for the first time. Official: [Twilight Trails](https://pokemongolive.com/seasons/twilight-trails).

### 7.9 Party Play

Level **21+** (official help; some old guides say 15 — trust the app). 2–4 trainers **physically nearby**. Shared challenges. Raid **Party Power** fills from Fast Attacks, then the next Charged Attack can deal **double** damage. **Party Share** (since 24 Sep 2024): share Incense / Lucky Egg / Star Piece; **duration is halved** for everyone; sharer limit 4×/day per item type. Kids: host must already be a friend; avatars/location are hidden.

Official: [What is Party Play?](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/4310-what-is-party-play/), [Party Share](https://pokemongo.com/post/partyshare).

### 7.10 Pokémon HOME

One-way transfer via **GO Transporter** (Nintendo Account + HOME tutorial). Costs Transporter Energy (recharges over time, or PokéCoins). **Cannot return to GO.**

Cannot send (non-exhaustive): Shadows, Mega-Evolved, buddy, gym defenders, many costumes, Spinda, favorites, **Gigantamax-capable**, Hyper-trained, some Safari Ball catches. Dynamax *can* go but **loses Max Moves**. Lucky/Purified tags are lost. HOME v4.0.0 (Apr 2026) added **Legends: Z-A**; moving into Z-A can lock a Pokémon out of older games. **GO Park** (Let’s GO) is a separate Kanto-only Bluetooth path and yields a Mystery Box (Meltan).

Official: [HOME transfer page](https://home.pokemon.com/en-us/transfer_from_go/), [GO help: sending to HOME](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/2787-sending-pokemon-to-pokemon-home/).

## 8. Monetization (what is actually worth buying)

**Usually worth it if you play a lot**

- Pokémon and item **storage**
- **GO Pass Deluxe** (~$8) if you will finish the track. Read it: Super Rocket Radar, Timed Incubator, Latios/etc. encounters, gift-open caps. Retroactive Deluxe claims exist. Help: [What is the GO Pass?](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/4972-what-is-the-go-pass/)
- **Community Day / GO Fest / Wild Area** tickets if you will be outside for the event. Global Fest 2026 was free; in-person and Wild Area Global are still ticketed
- Occasional **remote raid** passes if you cannot physically raid
- **Incubators** only when a 10/12 km pool is actually good (Deluxe Timed Incubator often covers this)
- **Link Charge bundles** only if you Super-Mega chain and free weekly charges are gone

**Usually not worth it**

- Random shop boxes of Poké Balls
- Powering up with paid Stardust (does not exist — people mean Star Pieces; those *are* good during 2×/3× Stardust events)
- Buying Max Particles / raid passes you will not use the same week

**Free coins:** 50/day from gyms is the whole F2P shop. That is slow by design.

Web store deals are often better priced than in-app. Still optional.

## 9. A sane weekly loop

**Daily**

1. Open gifts / send gifts (cap applies; do it with a Star Piece if you have a stack).
2. Field Research + Research Breakthrough if ready.
3. Daily Adventure Incense (bird hunt / extra catches).
4. Spin gym for free raid pass; use it on a worthwhile raid or Max Battle.
5. Buddy hearts (feed, snapshot, play, walk).
6. Optional: GBL sets for Stardust.

**Weekly** (when Daily Discoveries are on — skip this table during Fest/Tour/Mega Finale)

- Sun Routes / Mateo · Mon Max Battles · Tue Showcases · Wed Raid Hour · Thu 10 GBL sets · Fri in-person trades
- Hit **50 km** Adventure Sync if you can.
- Rocket Leaders for 12 km eggs and Shadow legendaries when in rotation.
- Party Play a raid if you have local friends.
- Ambassador Campfire check-in if your city has one (Timed Research / Link Charges; claim the first XP quickly).

**Monthly**

- Community Day: catch, check IVs *before* evolving, evolve in the move window.
- GO Pass: decide free vs deluxe based on the paid track, not FOMO.

## 11. Safety and account hygiene (short)

- Play somewhere you would walk anyway. Do not trespass, stop in traffic, or play while driving.
- Kids under the local digital-consent age **must** use Niantic Kids. Parent Portal gates friends, trading, Party Play, AR camera, and purchases. [Kids parent guide](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/5080-niantic-kids-child-account-parental-control-guide/).
- Link a **second login provider** (Google/Apple) and enable MFA on Trainer Central. PTC-only accounts are painful to recover ([Error 15](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/faq/4787-updating-your-pokemon-go-login-method/) outages happen). Facebook login is still supported but historically fragile.
- Scopely has said GO player data stays on US servers and is **not shared with Niantic Spatial**. Privacy policy last updated **20 Aug 2026**. Adventure Sync still reads Health/Fit.
- Do not share trainer codes in public with your real-time location. Campfire Team Up shares display name / profile photo in flare chat.
